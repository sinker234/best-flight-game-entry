TAILWIND — Seaside Air Rally: a complete flight game for make.fun's "Make the best flight game" bounty. Fly the Gullwing TW-1 prop plane in chase view down a 5-nautical-mile seeded coast — sea, curving beach, hills, a mountain wall, the town of Seabreeze with its lighthouse and 3 wind turbines — through 12 glowing numbered gold rings (+250 each), then a final approach and landing on Runway 27 (+1,000, time bonus up to +600, smooth-landing bonus +500). The rings flow in gentle steps (never more than 150 ft between neighbours, so no ring forces a steep dive), and a 1,200 ft stopway past the far end of Runway 27 catches a long landing instead of a terrain wall. Crash on terrain or sea, slam the runway, or overshoot and the flight is over. Cockpit instruments lit throughout: digital ALT / SPD / HDG, attitude indicator, airspeed and altitude tapes, heading compass strip, throttle bar, vertical speed, and a 12-dot course strip. Keyboard (arrows/WASD bank & pitch, Shift/Ctrl throttle, Space, P pause, M mute) or mouse steering, synthesized WebAudio engine/wind/chimes with no audio files. Built from scratch in one agent session — no libraries, no network requests, plain static files under game/.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.
